Project information

Latitude / longitude are project-location metadata only. All site geometry is designed in metres — geographic coordinates alone are not sufficient for centimetre-accurate site design without a GIS projection.

Land boundary

Click on the canvas to place boundary points. Segment lengths are shown live on the plan. The polygon area is computed with the shoelace formula.

Import parcel (GeoJSON / coordinates)

Copy the parcel geometry from any GIS source (Suhail, Balady, Google Earth, QGIS…). Coordinates are projected to metres locally — the boundary, area and location metadata are set automatically and the parking layout regenerates. After importing, assign your roads to the correct edges.

Roads

Assign each road to a land edge. Roads narrower than their minimum permitted width are highlighted on the plan and reported as warnings.

Building

Width and depth are derived from the required area and the preferred aspect ratio.

Drag the building on the plan to move it; use the corner handles to resize and the top handle to rotate. The plan and parking layout regenerate automatically.

Access & circulation

When enabled, 90° stalls are placed along the street frontage — including in front of the building when it stands near the road — nosed in straight from the public street. The entrance throat and building clearances stay free, and the rest of the site is planned normally.

Municipality presets

Presets store parking ratios, stall sizes, accessible / EV requirements, setbacks, coverage and road rules. They are saved in this browser only (localStorage). No unverified legal values are hard-coded — every field is editable.
